The Orbs

Not cutters — ornaments. Each orb is a few lines of pattern grammar in bikar, a text DSL for Islamic geometry, compiled straight to a watertight printable mesh: star patterns inscribed on a polyhedron's faces, projected to the sphere, and solidified into struts or woven ribbons. The rosette orbs are adjustable — open one in the Orb Lab to dial radius, petals, and struts, then download your own STL.
