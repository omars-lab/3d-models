How far to trust this

This is the slicer's plan, not a print. A bare spot in the plan may close when the first layer squashes, and the plan says nothing about how the plastic flows. How well the plan matches the bed face of a real piece is what the stock-against-house coupon print will measure.

The pass marks () were set on ten slices at the 0.4 nozzle. At 0.2, 0.6 and 0.8 they are the same marks applied as they are, not marks fitted to those nozzles. At 0.8 a line is wider than the 0.5 mm a slit may be, so a gap between two lines there is more likely to count as wide bare than as a slit.

Only the 0.4 nozzle is fitted on the printer. The other three nozzles' slices use Bambu's own presets for them, with the house changes made the same way: concentric first layer, 30% overlap, and a first line as wide as the nozzle's ordinary line. Slices made with tools/bed_face_sweep.py run.
