**claude** · 2026-10-01T15:54:07.777Z

Good call, these are feel questions. I'm writing a short design for sampler sheets. Each sheet is one question (edge, star tips, soft weld, top). Rows are the technique values and columns are small cut-out pieces of a pattern, e.g. a star point, a crossing, gBV's small star. Every sample is labeled with engraved text, on a tab or a backing card. It will say which samples can print today and which wait on bikar work. The edge fix is already being built. I'm holding the four calls until you have the sheets.

**claude** · 2026-10-01T16:12:10.688Z

The sampler design is written and merged (3d-models #463): design/coaster/sampler-sheets-design. Four sheets, each a labeled card of 30 mm windows cut from the real 90 mm coasters (CS-1, CS-2, gBV side by side). Sheet 1 is edge + top (today's edge, the true edge at round 1, the true edge at round 1.5, a finer grid). Sheet 2 is star points, sheet 3 is the soft weld, sheet 4 is the gBV fit gaps. Short codes are engraved on the card, and the values are on the plate's review page. None can print yet: they need the true edge (bikar, in progress now) and a window option. Three calls are yours, in its last section: how samples are held and labeled, what goes on sheet 1, and the sample size. Your vault copy doesn't have the page yet: the stray c on line 4 of the top-three page and this comment's anchor on this page keep it from updating. I'm holding the four smoothing calls on this page until the sheets print.

**claude** · 2026-10-01T17:53:50.070Z

The edge fix is merged (bikar #291): option 3 on this page is built. Sheet 1 now needs only the window cut and its card, plus the old staircase for its 'today's edge' row, which comes from the commit before the fix. The four calls here stay held for the sheets.

**claude** · 2026-10-01T20:01:43.881Z
