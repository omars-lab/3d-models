**claude** · 2026-10-01T16:42:30.723Z

Yes, prints should drive these. Calls 3 (raised fills) and 4 (the gaps) can be settled by a print, so I'm adding them as sampler sheets next to the other four (design/coaster/sampler-sheets-design): a fill-height sheet (lowered, flush, raised) and the gBV fit sheet at four gaps. Each gets a plate page in plates/ so it sits in the print queue, waiting on the bikar work it needs and on your send. Call 2 (where the guided page lives) is a screen question, not a print, so it gets a picture of each option instead. I'll reply here with the PR.

**claude** · 2026-10-01T16:58:20.902Z

Done in 3d-models #464. Each open call now says what decides it. Call 4 (the gaps) is sheet 4, the gBV fit: 0.05/0.10/0.15/0.20 mm on one ring, plus the small stars at 0.15. Call 3 (raised fills) is a new sheet 5: lowered 0.6, flush 1.2 and raised 1.8 on the same three windows, plus a tall loose piece. Raised against tall piece is call 3, and lowered against flush also settles the multicolor flush-or-lowered call. Call 2 has one picture per option (guided mode in the Lab, a separate page, a written how-to). Scheduled: plates/sheets-01 to sheets-05 are in the print queue as planned, each listing the build it waits on. Highest value first: sheets-04, 01, 05, 02, 03. Nothing is ticked and nothing is sent. Prints stay held for last and the send is yours. Your vault copy still lags master, see the other thread.

**claude** · 2026-10-01T20:01:44.383Z

The window cut is on bikar main (#293, 2026-10-01), and it works on sheet 5's square slab coaster, which was the untried case. The centre star cut at fill 1.2 (flush) and 0.6 (lowered) each comes out as one solid piece and passes the mesh check, and it splits into a body per color for a two-color print. Sheet 5 still needs the card and the sheet plate. Its raised row needs raised fills, and its tall-piece row needs a height option on loose pieces. Sheet 4 (the fit) doesn't use the window; it needs its own file and plate. Recorded in 3d-models #469.
