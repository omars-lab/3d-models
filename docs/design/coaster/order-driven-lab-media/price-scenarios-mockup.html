Same formula, every row

cost each     = order cost ÷ coasters (§9.3)
margin        = price × (1 − fee %) − fixed fee each − cost each
to cover     = ⌈ monthly fixed costs ÷ margin ⌉
revenue a month = price × X  ·  margin a month = margin × X
profit a month  = margin a month − monthly fixed costs
