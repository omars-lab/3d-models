# minis-04 — minis-03 at 80 mm and half the height, plus the twist

**In short.** Printed once, on 2026-09-26. The minis-03 patterns at twice the span and half the
height, a dovetail pair at clearance 0.10, and a CS-1 twist. Omar's verdict: tiny holes in the
