# Candidate screen A, 2026-09-27

New construction candidates from creators other than Sarah Brewer, screened for the
[catalog-expansion backlog](../tasks/catalog-expansion/backlog.md) item 2. Every
construction in the [ledger](../constructions/ledger.md) so far is Brewer's and is mostly
6-, 8- or 12-fold, with one 7-fold. What we want next is new fold counts (5, 7, 9, 10, 16)
and tilings, not more 8-fold.

This is one of two independent screens. It was written without reading the other one.

## How the screen was done

- **No video was downloaded.** For each candidate I read its YouTube metadata (title,
  channel, publish date, length, description) and looked at its storyboard sprite sheets.
  These are the small preview frames YouTube shows when you scrub the timeline. They were
  enough to see whether the steps are shown one by one and what the final pattern looks
  like. They are low resolution (roughly one frame every 1–10 s, depending on length), so
  a verdict on legibility is a judgment from small frames, not from watching the video.
- **License check.** YouTube marks a Creative Commons video in its watch page. I checked
  each page for that marker. I tested the check on one known CC source (Blender's Big Buck
  Bunny uploads), where it found the marker. It found none on any candidate here, so each
  one is **presumed** to carry the Standard YouTube License: we can watch it and rebuild the
  construction from it, but not download or republish the video. One positive control is
  thin evidence; treat "no CC" as likely, not proven.
- **GeoGebra files.** GeoGebra's terms (fetched) license user-uploaded materials under
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with credit to GeoGebra and the author and a link back. **NC means no
  commercial use** without a separate license. If the coasters are ever sold, a rebuild
  that copies a GeoGebra file (rather than re-deriving the construction from the video)
  carries that risk.
- **Coaster fit** follows the ledger's own precedents: bknVRSMcLj0's whole wall read as a
  solid slab at 90 mm, so its coaster is one cell (CS-12); n3IidKfXE1I's full field was
  near-solid, so its coaster is the rosette only. "Open" below means the final line work
  leaves most of a 90 mm disc as gaps, judged from storyboard frames.

## Hedges

- **Fold counts are read from storyboard frames and titles**, not from finished rebuilds.
  #2 is titled "4 Fold Rosette with 16 Petals": its 16 petals sit on a 32-division circle
  and the title calls the symmetry 4-fold; the rebuild will settle which it is.
- **Coaster fit is a guess from small frames.** The ledger shows twice that a pattern that
  looks fine in a video can read as solid at 90 mm. Each GO still needs the normal render
  and look before it is printed.
- **Licenses are presumed, not confirmed.** No candidate showed the CC marker, and no
  creator page I fetched (samiramian.uk /youtube and /fivefold) states a license. Rebuilding
  a construction from a video is how the Brewer set was made; downloading or republishing
  the videos is not allowed under the presumed license.
- **Method lineage.** #5 teaches Eric Broug's published method; #4 credits Aljanabi and
  Sarvdalir. A geometric construction method is generally not what copyright protects, but
  the book text and diagrams are; credit the source in the ledger row.
- **Scope of the search (K2).** Searches covered YouTube tutorials for 5-, 9-, 10- and
  16-fold rosettes and girih, the channels listed in the sources, and GeoGebra materials
  search. Within that, no 9-fold tutorial from a non-Brewer creator turned up. That says
  nothing about creators outside these searches, and paid course sites (Art of Islamic
  Pattern, Broug's school, Aljanabi's courses) were seen only as snippets.

## Recommendation: top 3, in reconstruction order

1. **NtnlGMTElBk** (Samira Mian, 10-fold). The cleanest steps found: a digital animation
   with nothing hidden by hands, ending in an open star. It is the first 10-fold. Being
   82 s long with no narration, read it alongside 0ke_GpoBa-s, which explains the 5- and
   10-division in words.
2. **88q-u2eWZqg** (Eman Zainab, 16 petals). A fold count the set lacks, with digital
   inserts that show the 32-division, and an open ring that should fit a disc.
3. **n_ICgwOr6qs** (Samira Mian, 5-fold). The only true 5-fold found. It is last of the
   three because it is built from arcs with a woven band, so it likely needs the arc path
   (nmEjCTzMbDg's) and may render denser than it looks.

Next in line: fhGHzop7ULw (Aljanabi) for its rectangle repeat unit, if a tiling is wanted
before another new fold; then A9fefFurD_s or LoCRh3SOhls as further 10-fold variants.
