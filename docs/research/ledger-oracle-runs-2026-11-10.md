## gBV_JTt3Kxk: what the 47 are

None of the 47 is a missing comparison. They fall into three kinds, each with the reason O1
printed:

- **36 edges GeoGebra makes on its own** ("auto-generated by GeoGebra"): when a construction
  draws a polygon, GeoGebra adds a segment for each side. The polygon itself is compared by its
  vertices, so its sides add nothing. They are `v0`–`v9` (the two blue pentagons),
  `f g i j k l m n p q` (the pink polygon), `f1 a1 a2`, `f2 a3 a4`, `f3 a5 a6`, `f4 a7 a8` (the four `mk`
  triangles) and `r l_1 s r_1` (the tile).
- **3 repeat patterns** ("a Sequence orbit is the naqsh pattern layer"): `blacks`, `wlow`,
  `ring`. These are the rotated copies the coaster draws. The points each repeat is built from
  are compared one by one; O2 and O3 check the repeated drawing.
- **8 extras on the naqsh side** ("conjugate of … under wlow (lowering)"): `W1_wlow` … `W6_wlow`,
  `Q_wlow`, `V9_wlow`. The importer makes these when it turns the `wlow` repeat into naqsh; they
  have no GeoGebra object of the same name.

The ten arcs (`tick1`–`tick4`, `arcM`, `arc0`, `arc2`, `arc4`, `arc6`, `arc8`) are compared, all
PASS by "host+sweep" (the circle they sit on and the angle they span).
