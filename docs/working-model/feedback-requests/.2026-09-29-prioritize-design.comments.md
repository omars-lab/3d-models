**claude** · 2026-10-01T00:05:38.124Z

Correction to my reply above: CS-6's seven petals are not uneven. The video's construction is exactly seven-fold, and so is our rebuild. The 0.73 came from the symmetry check turning the art about the middle of its bounding box, which on a seven-point star is about 2 mm off its real centre. bikar made the same mistake in the coaster itself, so the star printed 1.94 mm low in its heptagon frame (5.6 mm of margin at the top, 1.2 mm at the bottom). Both are fixed: bikar #290 centres the art on its true rotation centre (only CS-6's file changed), and 3d-models #453 makes the check turn about the centre of mass. CS-6 now scores 1.00. The off-centre star may be what felt off on minis-03, but that is not checked against the printed piece. Write-up: docs/issues/cs6-symmetry-centre.md.
