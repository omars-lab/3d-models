**claude** · 2026-10-01T14:44:37.332Z

Read. Two prints from one drawing: the lines alone (gBV's openwork coaster already exists in bikar), and the spaces as separate pieces in a second color. The loose-pieces design (docs/design/coaster/loose-pieces-design.md) covers this: it works on any pattern, ring by ring or every space at once. Will they fit? Not reliably today. Every slanted wall is a 0.4 mm staircase (smooth-lines design), and that is bigger than the gap a fit needs (about 0.15 mm a side, not yet measured). The sharp tips, especially gBV's small five-point stars, catch first. To make them fit, cut both parts from the same outline, so they match by construction. Make the walls true, take the whole gap off the pieces, and round the tips a little. Then a sample prints one frame plus pieces at four gaps, and the pieces that drop in without force give the number. Questions in chat.
