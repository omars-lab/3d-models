Omar liked the printed cedar-stencil-1 card's thickness: 0.6 mm, three 0.2 mm layers, bends and
springs back ("i like the thickness of the print", 2026-10-30). He had asked for "thin, maybe a few
layers, not to thick, still bendable", and the reading of "a few" as three was right.

**Why:** a confirmation, so the next stencil does not re-ask or drift thicker for safety.

**How to apply:** 0.6 mm is the default for any tracing card (the make-stencil skill says so). Go
to 0.8 mm only after a print shows creasing or torn slivers; 0.4 mm only if he says too stiff.
