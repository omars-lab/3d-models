When a peer session relays a decision of Omar's, record only the words it quotes from him, and
keep any wider claim it adds as the peer's reading until Omar says it himself.

**Why:** on 2026-11-14 the storefront session relayed Omar's "seperate tools" call and added that
the two hubs stay separate "for good" and that a later join was "off the table". I put both claims
into bikar's side bar note (bikar #382) and our done list (#1046). The peer then wrote back that
those were its reading, not his words, and both had to be taken back out (bikar #383, #1047). This
is K1 from CLAUDE.md, carrying a qualifier, applied to a relay: hardening "keep them separate"
into "never join" strips the open question.

**How to apply:** quote his words with their date. Where the peer goes further than his words, write
"asked, not answered yet", or leave the doc alone until the answer arrives. A relay that says
"nothing to reply" still gets read for which words are his.
