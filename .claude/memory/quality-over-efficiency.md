Quality over efficiency: the product is the coaster in the hand. A way that is quicker to build or
print but looks or feels worse is never the default or my pick, and its option names what it gives
up next to what it saves. Now a corollary under "Robustness over ease" in 3d-models CLAUDE.md (#597).

**Why:** I recommended way c (print-in-place pocket split) "for the quickest print". Its face prints
one color, with the pattern only as hairline gaps. Omar: "we are sacrificing quality", then "there
should be a tenet of quality over efficiency in our claude.md".

**How to apply:** when ranking options for a print or design, rank by how the finished piece looks
and feels first. Speed, filament and fewer calls are tie-breakers, never the lead reason. Related:
[[look-before-you-print]], [[omar-working-preferences]], [[color-switching-costs-time-and-filament]].
