When a call is about how pieces join, hold or fit (studs, pockets, glue, pegs, a stud layout),
offer a try-out plate that prints the candidates side by side, and let Omar decide from the pieces
in his hand. Don't make him pick on paper.

**Why:** On 2026-10-08 Omar was asked to pick on paper twice, and both times he turned it into a
try-out. On call 12, the stud count: "can we not expierment with stud setup? have dedicated plate
for this?" That became the stud-layout wedges. On call 15, the way-c join: "can we not try
different approaches and decide? first ... for peices in this situation ...". That became pkt-2,
after pkt-1.

**How to apply:**
- Put "try them in print" as an option whenever a join or fit call has two or more real
  candidates, with the plate's cost and what it waits on.
- Order the try-out so each print answers one question. pkt-2 is built at whichever air gap pkt-1
  shows comes free.
- Looks and colors are a different kind of call: Omar left the way-c face colors (15b) to be
  decided once a coaster exists.
